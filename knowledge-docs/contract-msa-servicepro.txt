# Master Services Agreement (MSA) — Meridian Manufacturing & ServicePro Staffing Solutions

**Agreement ID:** MSA-2025-0417
**Effective Date:** April 17, 2025
**Term:** 24 months, auto-renewing annually unless terminated with 60 days' notice
**Parties:** Meridian Manufacturing Inc. ("Client") and ServicePro Staffing Solutions LLC ("Vendor")

## 1. Scope of Services
Vendor provides contract labor (machine operators, warehouse associates, quality inspectors) to Client's Columbus, OH and Querétaro, MX production facilities.

## 2. Standard Labor Rates (Schedule A)
| Role                     | Standard Hourly Rate (USD) | Overtime Multiplier |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------|----------------------|
| Machine Operator I        | $24.50                      | 1.5x                 |
| Machine Operator II       | $28.75                      | 1.5x                 |
| Warehouse Associate       | $19.00                      | 1.5x                 |
| Quality Inspector         | $31.00                      | 1.5x                 |

## 3. Overtime Policy (Section 3.2 — binding clause)
- Overtime hours (hours worked beyond 40/week per worker) are billed at 1.5x the Standard Hourly Rate listed in Schedule A.
- **Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by a Meridian Manufacturing site supervisor before the shift is worked.** Verbal approval or after-the-fact approval does NOT qualify for overtime billing under this Agreement.
- Overtime hours billed without a corresponding written pre-approval record are **non-billable** and must be excluded from invoices, per Section 3.2(c).
- Monthly overtime hours billed to Client shall not exceed 10% of total standard hours billed for that site in the same month, unless an exception is jointly signed by both parties (Section 3.2(d)).

## 4. Invoicing & Audit Rights
- Vendor shall submit itemized monthly invoices within 10 business days of month-end, referencing worker ID, role, standard hours, overtime hours, and the written approval reference number for any overtime line.
- Client reserves the right to audit any invoice against timesheets and approval records within 90 days of receipt (Section 4.3).
- Discrepancies identified during audit are creditable against the next invoice cycle (Section 4.5).

## 5. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Vendor must fill open requisitions within 5 business days.
- Fill-rate below 90% in any month triggers a 5% service credit on that month's invoice (Section 5.2).

**Signed:** Meridian Manufacturing Inc. (Procurement) / ServicePro Staffing Solutions LLC (Account Management)
